# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](#简体中文)

The iterx community adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, in every iterxlabs repository, issue, discussion and in the iterx community site.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, sexualized content, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior to the team at [iterx.ai/contact](https://www.iterx.ai/contact). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content and warn, suspend or ban participants who violate this code.

---

## 简体中文

iterx 社区在 iterxlabs 的所有仓库、issue、讨论以及词元开物社区网站中采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 作为行为准则。

简单说：友善、尊重，给出并接受建设性的意见，以社区利益为重。不接受骚扰、辱骂、性暗示内容，以及公开他人隐私信息。不当行为请经 [iterx.ai/contact](https://www.iterx.ai/contact) 报告，我们会尽快且保密地处理；维护者可以删除内容，并对违规者警告、暂停或禁止参与。
